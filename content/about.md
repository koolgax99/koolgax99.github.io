Hi folks! I am Nihar Sanda. I recently completed my Master's in Computer Science at Northeastern University's Khoury College (GPA 4/4), where my thesis — *Cross-Domain Knowledge Retrieval Using LLM-Enhanced Graphs* — explored how knowledge graphs and large language models can be combined for multi-hop scientific reasoning. I currently work as a Machine Learning Engineer at Northeastern's Institute for Experiential AI, building GraphRAG systems for biomedical knowledge aggregation. Before Northeastern, I earned my B.Tech in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Dharwad.

A data-science-focused version of my resume is available [here](/resumes/Nihar_Sanda_DS.pdf).
